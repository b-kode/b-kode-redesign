Access to green

3-30-300

Picture: per-building 3-30-300 score over Warangal — from worst (amber) to best (teal). Zoom in and click any building for its three rule scores.

The 3-30-300 rule says everyone should see at least 3 trees from their home, live in a neighbourhood with 30% tree canopy, and be within 300 m of a park. We score every single building in a city against all three rules — so you see not an average, but exactly which streets and homes are left out.

Strengths & innovation

  • Every building scoredEvery single building in the city gets its own score.
  • Trees, canopy and parksRule 3 counts nearby trees, Rule 30 the surrounding canopy, Rule 300 the distance to a park.
  • Overall and per-rule viewSee one overall score or each rule separately, to find where the shortfall comes from.
  • Globally applicableBuilt on open data, so it can be run for any city in the world.
“Access to green is not one distance to one park. 3-30-300 measures it at three scales — the trees you see from your window, the canopy around your neighbourhood and the park within walking distance.”
On measuring access to green
