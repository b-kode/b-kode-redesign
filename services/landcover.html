Land cover

High-Resolution Land Cover

Picture: 2 m land-cover classification over the Jordaan, Amsterdam — every rooftop, canal, courtyard and tree canopy individually resolved.

A 2-metre land-cover classification — buildings, paved ground, low and high vegetation, bare soil and water — built as direct model input for SOLWEIG and the Urban Weather Generator, not just a pretty basemap.

Strengths & innovation

  • Model-ready classesBuildings, paved ground, low vegetation, tree canopy, bare soil and water — the exact classes SOLWEIG and the Urban Weather Generator need, no reclassification step.
  • 2 m resolutionIndividual rooftops, courtyards and street trees are resolved — not smoothed into a single "urban" class.
  • City-wide coverageBuilt across the full urban extent, not just a pilot neighbourhood — this demo covers central Amsterdam end to end.
  • Remote-sensing derivedClassified from satellite/aerial imagery, so it can be regenerated anywhere without ground survey.
