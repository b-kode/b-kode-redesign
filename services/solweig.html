Thermal comfort

SOLWEIG 2m Thermal Comfort Heatmap

Picture: zoom-in on a 2 m UTCI map — hot streets in red, shaded courtyards and greenery cool.

High-resolution human thermal comfort modelling that integrates radiation, wind and shading from buildings and vegetation to calculate UTCI / WBGT.

Strengths & innovation

  • Very high resolution2-metre resolution captures hyperlocal variation — building shadows, tree canopies.
  • Physics-basedUses physical modelling (SOLWEIG), not statistical inference — a scarce capability in the market.
  • Wind effectsAccounts for wind sheltering and channelling by buildings and vegetation.
  • UHI & future scenariosUrban Heat Island effects and future climate scenarios are included.

Quote

“Local feels-like temperatures can differ by many degrees across a single square — knowing where the hot spots are is what makes a redesign genuinely resilient.”
On designing resilient public space

Demo

Real 2 m UTCI rasters for two demo cities. The city map shows the typical-day heat-stress choropleth per neighbourhood — click an outlined hex to open its 2 m SOLWEIG detail with the basemap underneath.
