Thermal comfort

High-resolution human thermal comfort modelling that integrates radiation, wind and shading from buildings and vegetation to calculate UTCI / WBGT.


Picture: zoom-in on a 2 m UTCI map — hot streets in red, shaded courtyards and greenery cool.



Very high resolution

2-metre resolution captures hyperlocal variation — building shadows, tree canopies.

Physics-based

Uses physical modelling (SOLWEIG), not statistical inference — a scarce capability in the market.

Wind effects

Accounts for wind sheltering and channelling by buildings and vegetation.

UHI & future scenarios

Urban Heat Island effects and future climate scenarios are included.


“Local feels-like temperatures can differ by many degrees across a single square — knowing where the hot spots are is what makes a redesign genuinely resilient.”
